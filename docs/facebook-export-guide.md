# How to download your Facebook archive for TimeCapsule

**Format required: JSON** (not HTML)

TimeCapsule never logs into Facebook and never uploads your archive. You download the official copy from Meta once, then open it locally in the app.

## Why

Facebook Memories only shows one day at a time. Your full archive lets TimeCapsule answer questions like “What was I doing every August 5 for the last 15 years?”

## Where

Official tool only: [facebook.com/dyi](https://www.facebook.com/dyi)  
or **Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download / Export your information**

## How (desktop recommended)

1. Log in to Facebook on a computer.
2. Click your profile picture (top right) → **Settings & privacy** → **Settings**.
3. Open **Accounts Center** (or “Your Facebook Information”).
4. Go to **Your information and permissions** → **Download your information** / **Export your information**.
5. Select your Facebook profile.
6. **Date range:** All time (or a long window).
7. **Format:** **JSON** ← critical.
8. **Media quality:** High.
9. Include at least: Posts, Photos and videos, Check-ins / Places.
10. Create / Start the export and confirm with your password.
11. Wait for the email or notification (minutes to a couple of days for large histories).
12. Download the ZIP before the link expires.

## Common issues

| Problem | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| You chose HTML | Request a new export and select **JSON** |
| Link expired | Request the archive again |
| Very large ZIP | Prefer desktop + stable connection; you can request fewer categories first |
| Empty or sparse result | Request again with a wider date range and more categories |

## Privacy note

The ZIP stays on your device. TimeCapsule processes it in the browser. There is no Facebook login inside the app and no server that receives your memories.
